Back stageWhat I did underneath

Research

With the second-line leads, I read through 3 months of escalated tickets. Most already had an answer in a procedure or a past ticket. The knowledge existed. The job was to get it to first line.

Design

An AI assistant inside the first-line agent's workflow. It answers from about 400 procedures and the history of resolved tickets, and it always shows its source.

Test and improve

We built a 150-question test set with second-line staff and agreed the pass mark before launch: 85% correct, 95% right source, zero data leaks. Then a 4-week pilot with 8 agents, and a weekly review of agents' ratings ever since. Most poor answers traced back to outdated procedures, so we fixed the procedures.

The fund wanted its own teams to be able to request automation, run it and keep building after the programme ended.

ApproachThe same five moves every time

  1. Discover

    Start with the people who use the service and the people who run it. Tickets, interviews, workshops.

  2. Reframe

    Turn pain themes into the right question before anyone proposes a fix.

  3. Design

    Journeys, concepts and a service blueprint that everyone in the room can read.

  4. Test

    Pilot with real users, against a pass mark agreed up front.

  5. Measure

    Same report, before and after. If the number has not moved, the work is not done.

then round again
